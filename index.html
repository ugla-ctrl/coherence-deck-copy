What It Is

A festival with real work at its center.

Part celebration, part ceremony, part summit. A curated gathering where the optimists of consciousness, technology, art, and regeneration come to enjoy life together and leave with real projects and businesses they can get involved in, building the world that is arriving.

What it is not

  • A music festival with talks bolted on
  • A transactional networking conference
  • A doom-and-gloom debate about AI
  • A crowd that disperses when it ends

What it is

  • Connect with lifelong partners, personal and business
  • Heal through transformational ceremony, movement practices, bio-hacking, and the bath house
  • Collaborate on what we build for the post-AI world
  • Celebrate with music, great food, and play

The Four Days

One theme a day. One arc across four.

Friday · Arrival

Opening

Gates open at noon. Land, settle into the RV Village or your downtown hotel, and find your people on the river trail through the Grove. Opening Ceremony at 6 in the Ceremony Garden to set the intention for the four days, dinner together under the pecans, then the first night of music on the Amphitheater Stage until midnight.

Saturday · Equinox

Consciousness after the AI Revolution

Dance party at dawn. Keynote & panel on conscious AI and the future of humanity after universal abundance. Into the Darkness Ceremony, then headline music on the river.

Sunday

Consciousness in Financial & Political Systems

Dance party at dawn. Keynote & panel: founders, funds and organizers of the new systems of finance and governance. Waves of Illumination Ceremony, then headline music on the river.

Monday · Full Moon · World Water Day

World Water Day

A slow day to foster connection. The Bath House guided inner-water cleanse. Keynote & panel on the technology of water, the Love Water Ceremony, closing dinner and acoustic dance set.

04 · Program
Event Information
The Four Ceremonies

One ceremony a day, in the garden by the spring.

Friday · 6 PM

Opening Ceremony

We arrive as strangers and leave the garden as a circle. A welcome to the land and the river, a blessing from the elders who hold this work, and the shared intention we carry for the four days.

Saturday · Equinox

Into the Darkness

On the day of balance between light and dark, we go inward. A guided descent into what we fear about the future and what we have not yet faced, so that what we build from here is built honestly.

Sunday

Waves of Illumination

What was met in the dark is brought into the light. Sound, breath and movement move through the garden in waves, clearing the old systems in us and making room for the ones we came to build.

Monday · Full Moon · World Water Day

Love Water

Under the full moon, on World Water Day, we close at the spring. A water blessing for the Colorado River and every watershed our guests go home to, and a sending-off for the year ahead.

Each ceremony is woven with the day's theme and led by ceremony holders from the IMXP & UNIFY community

The Question

What will humans do when goods and services are nearly free?

AI is accelerating the evolution of society, and human culture will have to adapt. We believe it can be the best thing that ever happens to humanity, if we decide what it is for. More time with family. More creativity. Deeper human connection, more celebration, play, and time in nature. Coherence is where we predict what is coming and start building the places people will land.

New money, seeking meaning

Austin startups raised a record $7.19B in 2025, led by AI. The people building the post-AI economy live here, and they are looking for more than things.

Spending shifts to wellbeing

The global wellness economy grew 7.9% in 2024, and wellness travel is outpacing travel overall. Gatherings that combine the two are where it lands.

Three worlds, one place

Ceremony and consciousness, AI and technology, music and celebration. Austin is known for each one on its own, never for all three together.

The Place

Carson Creek Ranch.

Held by one family on this river since 1851.
  • 60 acres on the banks of the Colorado River, in Austin, TX
  • A Texas Century Ranch, stewarded by the same family for 150 years
  • Amphitheater Stage on the water, under old pecans: music, yoga, keynotes
  • The Ceremony Garden next to the property's spring for the daily ceremonies and deep practice
  • The Grove: a river trail of food vendors, artisans, biohacking, lounges, and The Bath House
  • Held on the Spring Equinox, through the Full Moon, closing on World Water Day

Access

A destination gathering without the journey.

Most transformational gatherings ask for two travel days before anyone arrives. Carson Creek Ranch is 15 minutes from downtown Austin and 5 minutes from Austin-Bergstrom International airport, so a guest can land and be on the land the same hour. Lower friction, higher conversion, national reach.

Zero travel days: fly in the morning, be in ceremony that afternoon. Nonstop flights from both coasts, most under four hours, into the conscious-builder hub itself.

SXSW's final weekend. SXSW runs March 15 to 21, so fans, artists and partners are already in town, and Austin locals get the non-corporate gathering on the river.
