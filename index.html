The Place

Carson Creek Ranch.

Held by one family on this river since 1851.
  • 60 acres on the banks of the Colorado River, in Austin
  • A Texas Century Ranch, worked by the same family for 150 years
  • Waterfront amphitheater under old pecans, 2,000 on grass terraces
  • Proven festival ground: camping, parking, power, permits, in place
  • Held on the Spring Equinox: balance, renewal, emergence
